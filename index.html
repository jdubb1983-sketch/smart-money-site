Be Smarter With Your Money

Be Smarter With Your Money is a YouTube channel with practical, honest ways to save money: groceries, bills, budgeting, saving habits, debt and smart shopping.

Episodes are written with AI assistance, checked for accuracy, and narrated with a synthetic voice. The channel owner reviews and approves every episode before it is published.

About the channel uploader

The channel uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished episodes, add captions and playlists to the owner's own channel, and publish them after the owner approves. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else and does not access any other person's data.
